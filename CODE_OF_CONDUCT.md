# Contributor Code of Conduct

ArchPulse participants commit to respectful, inclusive and constructive collaboration. Welcome contributors of varied experience; discuss technical ideas without personal attacks, harassment, insults, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, spam, or disclosure of confidential information.

**Expected behavior:** give actionable review feedback, explain trade-offs, assume good faith while remaining evidence-based, credit contributors, and de-escalate disputes. **Unacceptable behavior:** targeted harassment, hostile conduct, privacy violations, threats, and repeated bad-faith disruption.

## Enforcement

Report privately to the repository owner's verified GitHub contact or GitHub's abuse-reporting interface. Do not post private incident details publicly. The owner or a non-conflicted authorized maintainer will acknowledge when reasonably possible, investigate proportionately, restrict participation if necessary, and offer a chance to appeal to a non-conflicted reviewer if one becomes available. If no independent maintainer is available, disclose the conflict and avoid unilateral representation of neutrality.

Possible measures: reminder, warning, temporary restriction, permanent removal, or referral to platform moderation. No response deadline can be guaranteed. This project policy is original; it does not claim to incorporate an externally copyrighted covenant by reference.
