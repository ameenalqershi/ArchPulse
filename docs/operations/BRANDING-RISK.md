# Public name and branding risk — research observation, not legal advice

**Status: Unresolved / requires owner review.** The repository name `ArchPulse` is not evidence of exclusive trademark ownership.

A distinct open-source software product called **ArchiPulse** (with an additional letter `i`) is publicly published by DisruptiveWorks:

- https://github.com/DisruptiveWorks/archipulse
- https://archipulse.org/

The separate product also describes architecture visualization, AI/MCP integration and an Apache-2.0 license. The names and parts of the product positioning are similar enough to merit a legal/trademark and community confusion review. This is **not** a conclusion of trademark infringement, registration or priority.

Until reviewed:

1. Do not claim that ArchPulse is the world's first or that its name is unique.
2. Keep logos, icons, fonts, imagery and text independently developed, or include independently verified rights and provenance.
3. Do not imply affiliation with DisruptiveWorks, ArchiPulse or other competitors.
4. Document whether a rename or differentiating product descriptor would reduce confusion before launching a public brand or website.
5. Record the owner decision in a reviewed ADR; do not silently rename repository or change legal attribution.

Observed on 2026-11-10, without exhaustive trademark registry searches. This document is a cautious source-linked risk entry, not a comprehensive clearance report. References: S19, S21 and the project charter S00.
